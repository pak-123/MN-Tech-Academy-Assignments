Homemade Pizza Recipe!

Pizza is one of my favourite food.It is cheesy, tasty and easy to make at home. You can add your favorite topping and it with family and friend.

Ingredients:

  • Pizza dough.
  • Tomato sauce.
  • Cheese.
  • Vegetables (onion, Capsicum).
  • Chicken.

Cooking Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven.
  2. Spread tomato sauce on the dough.
  3. Add cheese and toppings.
  4. Bake for 15-20 minutes.
  5. Serve hot and enjoy.

Extra Topping and Sides:

  • Toppings:
    1. Olives.
    2. Mushrooms.
    3. Extra cheese.
  • Sides:
    1. Soft Drinks.
